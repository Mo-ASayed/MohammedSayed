# Apricot: ten small improvements, 19 October 2026

Each item is a separate implementation commit. The warm palette and the short explanation-before-experiment flow remain.

1. Progressive hints in the map, moving from concept to a practical nudge.
2. Full policy text behind map dots, including conditions and outdated status.
3. Undo for marker movement, search reach, freshness and context capacity.
4. Adjustable context window with an explicit two-unit challenge.
5. Document-cutting experiment: keep the refund condition and deadline together.
6. Inbox stress test: tune a threshold and see false alarms and missed spam.
7. Change the meeting date in source context and rebuild the scripted response.
8. Pin a setup and compare retrieval and context before and after a change.
9. Browser-only map progress, unlocked layer revisits and reset integration.
10. Tap-to-move interaction, keyboard position announcements and reliable focus after resets.

New experiments are optional disclosures beside the relevant concept. Models, scores, phrase budgets and scripted responses remain explicitly illustrative. No login or backend was added.

Validation includes production build, 24 unit checks, all existing browser suites, and a new browser suite covering the ten improvements, 320/390/768 layouts, touch input, blocked storage, reload/resume and resetting progress. CI runs the new suite automatically.
